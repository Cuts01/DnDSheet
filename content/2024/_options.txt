# Opciones que eligen los class features 2024 (resúmenes propios).
# Los Fighting Styles 2024 son feats (categoría fighting style) y se eligen de la lista de feats.
# Las listas que no están aquí (Arcane Shots, Elemental Disciplines...) se toman del pack 2014.

# ---------- Battle Master maneuvers ----------
@option maneuvers | Ambush | listname Maneuvers
Add a Superiority Die to a Dexterity (Stealth) check or an Initiative roll.
@option maneuvers | Bait and Switch | listname Maneuvers
Swap places with an ally within 5 feet; one of you adds the die to AC until your next turn starts.
@option maneuvers | Commander's Strike | listname Maneuvers
Give up one attack and a Bonus Action so an ally can use its Reaction to attack, adding the die to damage.
@option maneuvers | Commanding Presence | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to an Intimidation, Performance or Persuasion check.
@option maneuvers | Disarming Attack | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to damage; the target makes a Strength save or drops an object it holds.
@option maneuvers | Distracting Strike | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to damage; the next attack by someone else against the target has advantage.
@option maneuvers | Evasive Footwork | listname Maneuvers
As a Bonus Action, Disengage and add the die to your AC until your next turn starts.
@option maneuvers | Feinting Attack | listname Maneuvers
As a Bonus Action, gain advantage on your next attack against a creature within 5 feet and add the die to its damage.
@option maneuvers | Goading Attack | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to damage; the target makes a Wisdom save or has disadvantage attacking anyone but you.
@option maneuvers | Lunging Attack | listname Maneuvers
As a Bonus Action, Dash; if you then hit with a melee attack, add the die to damage.
@option maneuvers | Maneuvering Attack | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to damage; an ally can use its Reaction to move half its Speed without provoking from the target.
@option maneuvers | Menacing Attack | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to damage; the target makes a Wisdom save or is Frightened until your next turn ends.
@option maneuvers | Parry | listname Maneuvers
When hit by a melee attack, use your Reaction to reduce the damage by the die + your Strength or Dexterity modifier.
@option maneuvers | Precision Attack | listname Maneuvers
When you miss, add the die to the attack roll, possibly turning it into a hit.
@option maneuvers | Pushing Attack | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to damage; a Large or smaller target makes a Strength save or is pushed 15 feet.
@option maneuvers | Rally | listname Maneuvers
As a Bonus Action, an ally within 30 feet gains temp HP equal to the die + half your Fighter level.
@option maneuvers | Riposte | listname Maneuvers
When a creature misses you in melee, use your Reaction to attack it, adding the die to damage.
@option maneuvers | Sweeping Attack | listname Maneuvers
When you hit, another creature within 5 feet of you and the target takes the die as damage if your roll would hit it.
@option maneuvers | Tactical Assessment | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to a History, Insight or Investigation check.
@option maneuvers | Trip Attack | listname Maneuvers
Add the die to damage; a Large or smaller target makes a Strength save or has the Prone condition.

# ---------- Metamagic ----------
@option metamagic | Careful Spell | listname Metamagic
1 point: up to Charisma modifier creatures automatically succeed on the save and take no damage on a success.
@option metamagic | Distant Spell | listname Metamagic
1 point: increase the range by 60 feet, or make a touch spell reach 30 feet.
@option metamagic | Empowered Spell | listname Metamagic
1 point: reroll up to Charisma modifier damage dice. Can combine with another option.
@option metamagic | Extended Spell | listname Metamagic
1 point: double a duration of 1 minute or longer (up to 24 hours) and gain advantage on Concentration saves for it.
@option metamagic | Heightened Spell | listname Metamagic
2 points: one target has disadvantage on saves against the spell.
@option metamagic | Quickened Spell | listname Metamagic
2 points: cast an action spell as a Bonus Action, but not if you've already cast a level 1+ spell this turn.
@option metamagic | Seeking Spell | listname Metamagic
1 point: reroll a missed spell attack. Can combine with another option.
@option metamagic | Subtle Spell | listname Metamagic
1 point: cast without Verbal, Somatic or Material components (except costly ones).
@option metamagic | Transmuted Spell | listname Metamagic
1 point: change the damage type to Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning, Poison or Thunder.
@option metamagic | Twinned Spell | listname Metamagic
1 point: when a spell cast with a slot can target more creatures at a higher level, add one more target.

# ---------- Hunter ----------
@option hunters-prey | Colossus Slayer | listname Hunter's Prey
Once per turn, a weapon hit on a creature missing HP deals an extra 1d8.
@option hunters-prey | Horde Breaker | listname Hunter's Prey
Once per turn, make another attack with the same weapon against a different creature within 5 feet of the first.
@option defensive-tactics | Escape the Horde | listname Defensive Tactics
Opportunity attacks against you have disadvantage.
@option defensive-tactics | Multiattack Defense | listname Defensive Tactics
After a creature hits you, its other attacks this turn have disadvantage.

# ---------- Cleric, Druid ----------
@option divine-order | Protector | listname Divine Order
Training with Martial weapons and Heavy armor. {weapons martial} {armor heavy}
@option divine-order | Thaumaturge | listname Divine Order
One extra Cleric cantrip, and add your Wisdom modifier (minimum +1) to Intelligence (Arcana or Religion) checks.
@option blessed-strikes | Divine Strike | listname Blessed Strikes
Once per turn, a weapon hit deals an extra 1d8 Necrotic or Radiant damage.
@option blessed-strikes | Potent Spellcasting | listname Blessed Strikes
Add your Wisdom modifier to the damage of Cleric cantrips.
@option primal-order | Magician | listname Primal Order
One extra Druid cantrip, and add your Wisdom modifier (minimum +1) to Intelligence (Arcana or Nature) checks.
@option primal-order | Warden | listname Primal Order
Training with Martial weapons and Medium armor. {weapons martial} {armor medium}
@option elemental-fury | Potent Spellcasting | listname Elemental Fury
Add your Wisdom modifier to the damage of Druid cantrips.
@option elemental-fury | Primal Strike | listname Elemental Fury
Once per turn, a weapon or Wild Shape hit deals an extra 1d8 Cold, Fire, Lightning or Thunder damage.
